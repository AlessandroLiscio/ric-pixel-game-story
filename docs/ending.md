# Ending: the credits scene

The `ending` scene (`src/game/ending/index.ts`, default and named export `EndingScene`, registered by `src/game/flow/sceneList.ts` through its glob) is where the story ends: the game goes to it straight after the Cortile (the night scene at Casa Circe is no longer played as a room, its lines are spoken during the credits), and it hands back to `title`.

The picture is the real Casa Circe living room at night: the tile map of the divano scene (`src/data/maps/divano.json`, drawn by `scripts/art/rooms/divano.py`, see the divano section of `docs/tiles.md`), drawn with `TileWorld` from the top left corner of the screen, so a map pixel is a screen pixel, with its night mood, its fairy lights and its dust. Its tiles sort under depth 400, its night overlay is at 750, its light glows at 760 and its dust at 770, so everything the scene adds is placed by the `DEPTH` object of `layout.ts` (the TV picture and the "30" balloons over the overlay, the credits column and the finale over everything). `layout.ts` reads the named points of the map (`tv`, `seat_*`), so moving a piece in the room script moves the ending with it; the only numbers copied by hand are the position of the screen inside the TV stamp (16, 6, 96 x 54, `SCREEN` in `scripts/art/stamps/divano.py`) and the small offsets of each seat. The room is arranged after the owner's floor plan (see docs/tiles.md): the TV stands in front of the stairs wall with its screen toward the camera and the navy sofa on the south wall faces it, seen from behind. Everything the ending shows sits left of the credits column (x < 400): the table at the left, the TV and the rug in the middle, the sofa under them; the sofa bed under the world map is behind the column.

The TV on its white cabinet plays a looping fish documentary (`tv.ts`): a 96x54 canvas laid exactly over the screen of the TV stamp, redrawn at about 12 frames per second with a clownfish and other small fish, a silver school, a manta ray far behind, jellyfish, a crab on the sand, swaying seaweed, light rays, bubbles and fake subtitles, under scanlines and a glare. The TV throws a soft additive glow that flickers (`ENDING_FX.tv` in `fx.ts` clips the pixelfx critters to the same rectangle).

The whole party is there at the scale of the tile world (32 px tall) with a one pixel breathing bob. They use the standing frame of each `char-<id>` sheet and sit by depth sorting against the tiles of the room: Liscio, Ric and Lara on the sofa in front of the TV (everyone is in the room for the whole night; whoever goes upstairs leaves only when the upstairs gag below starts), seen from behind (the up frame; the sofa tile, which has a higher depth, hides their bodies, so only the heads and shoulders show over the backrest), Giusy asleep on the white chair at the west side of the table (it faces east, the seat hides her legs), Ros on the clear chair at the east side, Lollino on the rug behind the pink pouf (it hides all but head and shoulders), Giuggiu asleep on her back under a blanket and a pillow in front of the coffee table, with `z` bubbles floating over the sleepy ones once the upstairs gag starts (before that they still talk), and the gold 30 balloons over the stairs wall. No minigame result changes the room or the lines; the run state on `game.registry` under `run` changes one thing, who goes upstairs (`flags.numeroFrangetta`, below).

The credits start with the scene: `credits.title` and `credits.lines` from `dialogue.it.json` scroll slowly upward over a dark column on the right, in the pixel font. A line of the form `Name ... role` is shown as a gold name over a pale role, any other line is plain white, and the last line "Buon compleanno, Ric!" is left out because the finale shows it big. The column goes by in 45 s (`SCROLL_MS` in `src/game/ending/script.ts`), and five times faster once the last spoken line has been read.

While the credits roll, the night scene of the divano room is spoken like any dialogue: the first line shows after 2.5 s, and each one stays until X, Enter or Space (a blinking arrow says so; Z does nothing here). The lines are those of `scenes.divano` (`arrival`, `meet`, `join`), then the upstairs gag (`buildBeats` in `script.ts`). `meet` is the TV exchange: the documentary praises its fish and ends on "... le acciughe!", and on the next line, the last of `meet`, everyone laughs: all who are in the room hop out of step with an "AH!" over their heads. Each line shows in a banner along the bottom of the room (a VX Ace window like the dialogue box, with the speaker's portrait and name, or the narrator's plain text in pale blue); the banner is 394 px wide with room for about 45 letters a row and grows upward, so keep a line under about 110 letters (`scripts/check-dialogue.mjs` enforces it for these lines). The speaker, when in the room, hops two pixels.

The upstairs gag comes last, and everything it implies is shown in the room: when its first line starts, the ones who go upstairs (`awayOf` in `script.ts`) get up, walk to the top of the stairs on the TV wall (`UPSTAIRS` in `layout.ts`, the dark landing at the left end of the steps) and fade out (`leave()` in `index.ts`, 1.8 s); only then pink hearts and pink "z" puffs drift up from there and a bed creaks (two low `audio.tone` blips every 0.72 s). Before the gag nobody is upstairs: no hearts, no creaks, and the sleepy ones show no `z` yet. By default Liscio and Lara go up and Ric says `ending.upstairs`. If `run.flags.numeroFrangetta` is true Ric goes up instead (the fringe girl waits up there) and Ros says `ending.upstairsFrangetta`. `ending.fromUpstairs` tags the name of a gag line spoken by someone who has gone up (none of the current lines).

Esc or C opens `MenuScene` over the credits (`scene.launch('menu', { from: 'ending' })`, then `scene.pause()`, the same way `WorldScene.openMenu` does), which pauses the roll, the creaks and the particles; closing the menu resumes the scene. The music does not pause. The menu works in the finale too.

The finale starts once the last spoken line has been confirmed and the last credits line has left the screen. The player sets that moment, so the `credits` track jumps to its Happy Birthday phrase there (`audio.playTrack('credits', BIRTHDAY_STEP)`, the pickup in the last beat of bar 16) and loops on from it. The column dims, "Buon compleanno Ric!" pops in big with a confetti burst, sparkles and a light confetti rain, about three seconds later `ui.theEnd` ("Fine", the label in `labels.ts` is only a fallback) fades in, and about two seconds after that "Premi X per ricominciare" blinks. Then X, Enter or Space stops the music, calls `resetRun` from `src/game/flow/RunState.ts` and fades to `title`. The Italian texts that the dialogue file does not hold are in `src/game/ending/labels.ts`.

Test builds only: the `test-skip` game event (what `window.__ricTest.skip()` emits) goes straight to the finale, and a second one shows the restart prompt at once. Nothing is added to `window`.
