# Ending: the credits scene

The `ending` scene (`src/game/ending/index.ts`, default and named export `EndingScene`, registered by `src/game/flow/sceneList.ts` through its glob) is where the story ends: the divano scene fades into it, and it hands back to `title`.

The picture is the real Casa Circe living room at night: the tile map of the divano scene (`src/data/maps/divano.json`, drawn by `scripts/art/rooms/divano.py`, see the divano section of `docs/tiles.md`), drawn with `TileWorld` from the top left corner of the screen, so a map pixel is a screen pixel, with its night mood, its fairy lights and its dust. Its tiles sort under depth 400, its night overlay is at 750, its light glows at 760 and its dust at 770, so everything the scene adds is placed by the `DEPTH` object of `layout.ts` (the TV picture and the "30" balloons over the overlay, the credits column and the finale over everything). `layout.ts` reads the named points of the map (`tv`, `seat_*`), so moving a piece in the room script moves the ending with it; the only numbers copied by hand are the position of the screen inside the TV stamp (16, 6, 96 x 54, `SCREEN` in `scripts/art/stamps/divano.py`) and the small offsets of each seat.

The TV on its white cabinet plays a looping fish documentary (`tv.ts`): a 96x54 canvas laid exactly over the screen of the TV stamp, redrawn at about 12 frames per second with a clownfish and other small fish, a silver school, a manta ray far behind, jellyfish, a crab on the sand, swaying seaweed, light rays, bubbles and fake subtitles, under scanlines and a glare. The TV throws a soft additive glow that flickers (`ENDING_FX.tv` in `fx.ts` clips the pixelfx critters to the same rectangle).

The whole party is there at the scale of the tile world (32 px tall) with a one pixel breathing bob. They use the standing frame of each `char-<id>` sheet, facing left toward the TV, and sit by depth sorting against the tiles of the room: Liscio and Ric on the sofa bed against the backrest (the quilt tile, which has a higher depth, hides their hips; Ros takes Ric's place when he is on the chair), Lara lying across the foot of the bed, Giusy asleep on the left white chair (the seat hides her legs), Lollino on the floor behind the pink pouf (it hides all but head and shoulders), Giuggiu asleep on her back under a blanket and a pillow in front of the coffee table, with `z` bubbles floating over the sleepy ones, and the gold 30 balloons by the shelves. The run state on `game.registry` under `run` changes two things: if `results['cat-smoke']` is true the cat `char-gatto` sleeps by the slippers, and if `results.slackline` is true Ric stands on the right chair on one foot (the step frames of the up row) and wobbles while Ros sits on the sofa bed instead; otherwise Ros sits on that chair and Ric is on the sofa bed.

The credits start with the scene: `credits.title` and `credits.lines` from `dialogue.it.json` scroll slowly upward over a dark column on the right, in the pixel font. A line of the form `Name ... role` is shown as a gold name over a pale role, any other line is plain white, and the last line "Buon compleanno, Ric!" is left out because the finale shows it big. The roll lasts 42 seconds (`CREDITS_MS`), so that the finale lands on the Happy Birthday phrase of the `credits` track (24 bars at 100 bpm, 57.6 seconds, started with `audio.playTrack('credits')` and looping). Holding X scrolls four times faster.

When the last line has left the screen the column dims, "Buon compleanno Ric!" pops in big with a confetti burst, sparkles and a light confetti rain, about three seconds later `ui.theEnd` ("Fine", the label in `labels.ts` is only a fallback) fades in, and about two seconds after that "Premi X per ricominciare" blinks. Then X, Enter or Space stops the music, calls `resetRun` from `src/game/flow/RunState.ts` and fades to `title`. The Italian texts that the dialogue file does not hold are in `src/game/ending/labels.ts`.

Test builds only: the `test-skip` game event (what `window.__ricTest.skip()` emits) ends the roll, and a second one shows the restart prompt at once. Nothing is added to `window`.
