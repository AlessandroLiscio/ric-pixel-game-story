# Ending: the night room and the skydive

The story ends in two scenes. The game goes to `ending` (Casa Circe late at night) straight after the Cortile (the night scene at Casa Circe is no longer played as a room, its lines are spoken there), `ending` hands over to `skydive` (the birthday present, with the credits and the finale), and `skydive` hands back to `title`.

## The night room (`ending`)

The `ending` scene (`src/game/ending/index.ts`, default and named export `EndingScene`, registered by `src/game/flow/sceneList.ts` through its glob) shows the real Casa Circe living room at night: the tile map of the divano scene (`src/data/maps/divano.json`, drawn by `scripts/art/rooms/divano.py`, see the divano section of `docs/tiles.md`), drawn with `TileWorld` from the top left corner of the screen, so a map pixel is a screen pixel, with its night mood, its fairy lights and its dust. Its tiles sort under depth 400, its night overlay is at 750, its light glows at 760 and its dust at 770, so everything the scene adds is placed by the `DEPTH` object of `layout.ts` (the TV picture and the "30" balloons over the overlay). `layout.ts` reads the named points of the map (`tv`, `seat_*`), so moving a piece in the room script moves the ending with it; the only numbers copied by hand are the position of the screen inside the TV stamp (16, 6, 96 x 54, `SCREEN` in `scripts/art/stamps/divano.py`) and the small offsets of each seat. The room is arranged after the owner's floor plan (see docs/tiles.md): the TV stands in front of the stairs wall with its screen toward the camera and the navy sofa on the south wall faces it, seen from behind. The table is at the left, the TV and the rug in the middle, the sofa under them and the sofa bed under the world map at the right. The music is the day track of Casa Circe (`circe`).

The TV on its white cabinet plays a looping fish documentary (`tv.ts`): a 96x54 canvas laid exactly over the screen of the TV stamp, redrawn at about 12 frames per second with a clownfish and other small fish, a silver school, a manta ray far behind, jellyfish, a crab on the sand, swaying seaweed, light rays, bubbles and fake subtitles, under scanlines and a glare. The TV throws a soft additive glow that flickers (`ENDING_FX.tv` in `fx.ts` clips the pixelfx critters to the same rectangle).

The whole party is there at the scale of the tile world (32 px tall) with a one pixel breathing bob. They use the standing frame of each `char-<id>` sheet and sit by depth sorting against the tiles of the room: Liscio, Ric and Lara on the sofa in front of the TV (everyone is in the room for the whole night; whoever goes upstairs leaves only when the upstairs gag below starts), seen from behind (the up frame; the sofa tile, which has a higher depth, hides their bodies, so only the heads and shoulders show over the backrest), Giusy asleep on the white chair at the west side of the table (it faces east, the seat hides her legs), Ros on the clear chair at the east side, Lollino on the rug behind the pink pouf (it hides all but head and shoulders), Giuggiu asleep on her back under a blanket and a pillow in front of the coffee table, with `z` bubbles floating over the sleepy ones once the upstairs gag starts (before that they still talk), and the gold 30 balloons over the stairs wall. No minigame result changes the room or the lines; the run state on `game.registry` under `run` changes one thing, who goes upstairs (`flags.numeroFrangetta`, below).

The night scene of the divano room is spoken like any dialogue: the first line shows after 2.5 s (`LEAD_IN_MS` in `script.ts`), and each one stays until X, Enter or Space (a blinking arrow says so; Z does nothing here). The lines are those of `scenes.divano` (`arrival`, `meet`, `join`), then the upstairs gag (`buildBeats` in `script.ts`). `meet` is the TV exchange: the documentary praises its fish and ends on "... le acciughe!", and on the next line, the last of `meet`, everyone laughs: all who are in the room hop out of step with an "AH!" over their heads. Each line shows in a banner along the whole bottom of the screen (`SpeechBanner` in `banner.ts`, placed by `SPEECH` in `layout.ts`; the skydive uses the same one; a VX Ace window like the dialogue box, with the speaker's portrait and name, or the narrator's plain text in pale blue) that grows upward with the rows; a line stays under 110 characters (`scripts/check-dialogue.mjs` enforces it for these lines and for those of the plane). The speaker, when in the room, hops two pixels.

The upstairs gag comes last, and everything it implies is shown in the room: when its first line starts, the ones who go upstairs (`awayOf` in `script.ts`) get up, walk to the top of the stairs on the TV wall (`UPSTAIRS` in `layout.ts`, the dark landing at the left end of the steps) and fade out (`leave()` in `index.ts`, 1.8 s); only then pink hearts and pink "z" puffs drift up from there and a bed creaks (two low `audio.tone` blips every 0.72 s). Before the gag nobody is upstairs: no hearts, no creaks, and the sleepy ones show no `z` yet. By default Liscio and Lara go up and Ric says `ending.upstairs`. If `run.flags.numeroFrangetta` is true Ric goes up instead (the fringe girl waits up there) and Ros says `ending.upstairsFrangetta`. `ending.fromUpstairs` tags the name of a gag line spoken by someone who has gone up (none of the current lines).

Once the last line has been confirmed and the hearts have run for 1.5 s (counted from the moment they start, so a quick reader still sees them), the scene fades to `skydive` (`fadeToScene` from `src/game/transitions`).

## The skydive (`skydive`)

The `skydive` scene (`src/game/ending/skydive.ts`, default and named export `SkydiveScene`, its pictures drawn in code by `sky-art.ts`) is a daytime sky with clouds, with no title card: the plane is the reveal. The `credits` track starts with the scene. A small propeller plane flies in from the left near the top, engine drone and all, and slows to a hover in the middle of the area left of the credits column (x < 400), with the heads of Liscio, Ric and Lara in its windows. The lines of `skydive.plane` in `dialogue.it.json` play in the same bottom banner as the night room: the first one comes after about 2.5 s, and each one waits for X. The credits do not roll yet.

After the last plane line the door opens and Lara, Ric and Liscio jump one after the other, each with a short shout in a bubble. About 3 s of belly-down freefall follow (clouds rush upward, the bodies wobble), then three parachutes open, each canopy in the colour of that person's shirt and Ric's with a "30" on it. The three drift down, swaying, while clouds scroll slowly upward so the fall reads as motion, and the plane flies off to the right.

The credits start at the jump: `credits.title` and `credits.lines` scroll upward over a dark column on the right, in the pixel font. A line of the form `Name ... role` is shown as a gold name over a pale role, any other line is plain white, and the last line "Buon compleanno, Ric!" is left out because the finale shows it big. The column goes by in a fixed 45 s (`SCROLL_MS` in `skydive.ts`), and holding X (or Enter or Space) makes it four times faster. The dark column fades away as the fields rise, and comes back faintly behind the finale.

When the last credits line has left the screen, green fields with the Bologna skyline far away (the Due Torri among it) rise from below and the three land on the grass. Then the finale plays: the `credits` track jumps to its Happy Birthday phrase (`audio.playTrack('credits', BIRTHDAY_STEP)`, derived in `src/game/audio/music/credits.ts`: the pickup in the last beat of bar 16) and loops on from it, "Buon compleanno Ric!" pops in big with a confetti burst, sparkles and a light confetti rain, then `ui.theEnd` ("Fine", the label in `labels.ts` is only a fallback) fades in, and then "Premi X per ricominciare" blinks. X then calls `resetRun` from `src/game/flow/RunState.ts` and fades to `title`. The Italian texts that the dialogue file does not hold are in `src/game/ending/labels.ts`.

The scene has public fields that the end-to-end test reads: `phase` (`plane`, `fall`, `land`, `finale`), `beats`, `beatIx` (-1 before the first plane line, `beats.length` once all are read) and `canRestart`.

## Menu and test skip

Esc or C opens `MenuScene` over either scene (`scene.launch('menu', { from })` with the key of the scene, `scene.bringToTop('menu')`, then `scene.pause()`), which pauses the scene with its timers and particles; closing the menu resumes it. `WorldScene.openMenu` needs no `bringToTop`, but the menu is registered before the two ending scenes and would be drawn under them without it. The music does not pause. In the skydive the menu works in the finale too.

Test builds only, through the `test-skip` game event (what `window.__ricTest.skip()` emits): in the night room it goes straight to the hand-over to `skydive`; in the skydive, during the plane it jumps to the jump (all lines read, phase `fall`), during the fall or the landing it goes straight to the finale, and in the finale one more skip shows the restart prompt at once. Nothing is added to `window`.
