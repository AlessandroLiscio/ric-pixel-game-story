# Ending: the credits scene

The `ending` scene (`src/game/ending/index.ts`, default and named export `EndingScene`, registered by `src/game/flow/sceneList.ts` through its glob) is where the story ends: the divano scene fades into it, and it hands back to `title`.

The picture is a dark living room at night, drawn entirely in code (canvas textures made once in `art.ts`, pixel rectangles only, so everything stays crisp at integer zoom). A big TV on a cabinet plays a looping fish documentary (`tv.ts`): a 112x72 canvas redrawn at about 12 frames per second with a clownfish and other small fish, a silver school, a manta ray far behind, jellyfish, a crab on the sand, swaying seaweed, light rays, bubbles and fake subtitles, under scanlines and a glare. The TV throws a soft additive blue glow and a light cone on the floor that flicker, and the characters are tinted with the same flickering light.

The whole party is there with the static idle frame facing up (frame 10 of each `char-<id>` sheet) and a one pixel breathing bob: Liscio, Lara and Ric on the sofa (Ros takes Ric's place when he is on the chair), Giusy asleep on the left chair, Lollino on a pouf on the floor (cropped to head and shoulders), Giuggiu asleep under a blanket on the rug, with `z` bubbles floating over the sleepy ones, leftovers of the party on the coffee table, bunting, gold 30 balloons, a bookshelf and a moonlit window. The run state on `game.registry` under `run` changes two things: if `results['cat-smoke']` is true the cat `char-gatto` sleeps beside the right chair, and if `results.slackline` is true Ric stands on the right chair on one foot (the step frames of the up row) and wobbles while Ros sits on the sofa instead; otherwise Ros sits on that chair and Ric is on the sofa.

The credits start with the scene: `credits.title` and `credits.lines` from `dialogue.it.json` scroll slowly upward over a dark column on the right, in the pixel font. A line of the form `Name ... role` is shown as a gold name over a pale role, any other line is plain white, and the last line "Buon compleanno, Ric!" is left out because the finale shows it big. The roll lasts 42 seconds (`CREDITS_MS`), so that the finale lands on the Happy Birthday phrase of the `credits` track (24 bars at 100 bpm, 57.6 seconds, started with `audio.playTrack('credits')` and looping). Holding X scrolls four times faster.

When the last line has left the screen the column dims, "Buon compleanno Ric!" pops in big with a confetti burst, sparkles and a light confetti rain, about three seconds later `ui.theEnd` ("Fine", the label in `labels.ts` is only a fallback) fades in, and about two seconds after that "Premi X per ricominciare" blinks. Then X, Enter or Space stops the music, calls `resetRun` from `src/game/flow/RunState.ts` and fades to `title`. The Italian texts that the dialogue file does not hold are in `src/game/ending/labels.ts`.

Test builds only: the `test-skip` game event (what `window.__ricTest.skip()` emits) ends the roll, and a second one shows the restart prompt at once. Nothing is added to `window`.
