# Scenes: layout decisions

`src/data/scenes.json` holds the six scenes in story order. Check it with `node scripts/check-scenes.mjs`: it prints `PASS scenes: 6 scenes, all positions valid` or the first 20 problems. All coordinates are in the 640x360 game space. Every person position (`spawn`, `friend`, `friend2`, `extras`) is the point where the character's feet touch the floor (sprite origin bottom centre), so it must lie inside a walk rectangle, and a 32x32 sprite stands in the box from (x-16, y-32) to (x+16, y). `facing` is `down`, `left`, `right` or `up`. Rectangles in `walk` limit where Ric's feet may go; together they form one connected area (rectangles overlap or touch), and the door touches it. The positions were checked by drawing the rectangles, door, spawn and people over the background PNGs at 2x. F9 in a dev or test build draws the same overlay in the running game: walk rectangles green, door red, spawn blue, friends yellow, extras magenta.

Schema beyond the contract, all optional except where stated. `title` (string, the Italian place name shown briefly on arrival) is required. `spawn` also has a `facing`. `door` is `null` in `divano` and otherwise `{x,y,w,h}` with an optional `draw: true`, which asks the flow to draw a plain door panel over the rectangle because the picture has no real door there (only `giuggiu`). `friend` is `null` in `divano`. `friend2` (circe only) is Giusy, the second friend who joins in that scene. `minigame2` (circe only) is `mash`, the second minigame after `d20`. An extra has `personId`, a position and `facing`, and one of: `egg` (an id of `easterEggs` in `dialogue.it.json`, played as a dialogue when Ric talks to it), `intro: true` (Prof. Pinolo: the flow plays the lecture lines of `meet` when Ric talks to him) or `sfx: "meow"` (the cat only meows). An extra may also have `tint` (`#rrggbb`) so one sprite can stand in for several passersby; the three egg extras use the `sconosciuta` sprite with a different tint because the cast has no sprite for a student, a dog walker and a barista. `ambient` entries are `{ type, preset, area: {x,y,w,h}, ... }`: type `particles` with preset `dust`, `pollen`, `steam` or `sparkle` (optional `count`); type `tween` with preset `flicker`, `sway` or `pulse` (optional `color`, `alpha: [min,max]`, `period` in ms), meant as a tinted rectangle or a gentle sway of the area. Presets are the vocabulary the ambient code must implement; an unknown preset is rejected by the validator.

Debug: `installDebug(game)` in `src/game/debug/index.ts` does nothing in a production build and the bundle contains no debug code. In the dev server and in `VITE_TEST_BUILD=1` builds it listens to the keys F1 to F6 (jump to scene 1 to 6, `debug:jump` with `{ sceneIndex }`), F7 and F8 (win and lose the running minigame, `debug:minigame` with `{ won }`) and F9 (overlay). The flow must listen to the two events on `game.events`; the overlay reads `run.sceneIndex` from `game.registry` and draws on the active scene with key `world`.

**universita.** The lecture hall is a steep amphitheatre, so the only open floor is the stage in front of the board, a strip of 184x38 pixels at the lower right. Prof. Pinolo stands at the lectern (feet at 425,222), Lollino is at (540,242) facing left and the student extra stands at the left end of the strip. The door is the wooden arch at the right edge, which touches the strip. The aisle at the lower left that the first sketch suggested was dropped: in the final picture it is dark benches with students, not floor. Ric starts at (480,250) between Pinolo and Lollino, which is tight but keeps the whole scene readable in one glance; the layout is acceptable, not generous.

**giuggiu.** The room has a parquet floor across the lower middle, three overlapping rectangles that go around the glass table and its chairs. Giuggiu stands to the right of the table near the shelves at (470,300) and the cat sits at the edge of the red sofa (330,334) so that the sofa reads as its seat. The picture has no door: the wall between the left wardrobe and the window is flat and empty, so the door is a drawn panel there (`draw: true`) reached by a pocket of floor at its foot, and the small rack that stands in front of that wall is simply covered by the panel. Ric enters from the left at (210,322). The floor is thin (about 22 pixels of free depth in front of the table), so the party train needs the two-row strip in the middle; it is the tightest scene after circe and divano.

**giardini.** All the grass is walkable except the trunks. The thin trunks at x 94-110, 150-170 and 198-208 are avoided by three rectangles: a full-width strip along the bottom, a large block on the right of the trunks and a small block between the first and the third trunk. The big trunk on the right edge (x 600-632) blocks that side, so the exit is the left edge of the park and the door is a 18x40 rectangle at the screen edge with no drawn door. Ric enters at (60,330), next to the exit, which means the player walks away from the exit to meet Liscio at (400,278), near the open sunlit grass in the middle where the slackline will be. The dog walker extra stands at (520,300).

**circe.** The photo is nearly all dinner table and chairs, so the only open floor is the light tile strip at the lower right, 200x38 pixels, between the chair legs and the metal bowl in the corner. Ros (500,308) and Giusy (566,314) stand on it side by side, both facing left toward Ric, who enters at (452,322). The white door at the back left is behind the table and cannot be reached, so the exit is the right edge of the room (a 16x34 rectangle at the screen edge, no drawn door). The silhouettes baked into the picture (five blurred people and the one standing at the right) stay as the background crowd; there are no extras in this scene. This is the weakest layout: the player can move only about 200 pixels, which is enough for two minigame hosts and a short walk.

**cortile.** The venue is dark, and the floor is the crowd band along the bottom, so a wide strip of 600x60 pixels is walkable (the crowd heads simply look like a dark floor) with a short column of 48x40 that leads up to the lit staircase on the left, the door. The staircase is at x 138-178, y 170-254, an orange lit pillar, and the picture shows it clearly. Lara stands in the crowd in front of the stage at (330,304) facing up, the barista extra at (470,306), and Ric enters at (190,300) close to the stairs, which gives a long walk to Lara across the venue. The stage lights pulse in the stage area.

**divano.** The final scene has no door and no friend: the flow plays `meet`, the easter eggs, `join` and then the credits. The photo has almost no floor, because the table fills the bottom and the sofa the middle, so the walkable area is a thin strip, 450x36, in the shadow gap in front of the sofa and over the grey foreground object at its left, from x 30 to x 480. The characters stand in front of the sofa with their feet at y 286-322; the party trails to the left of Ric, who starts at (330,302). The glass balcony door at the left is a good place for the girl upstairs (`sconosciuta`) to enter, but no extra is placed there because the `ric-sopra` egg is played by the flow without her sprite; an optional sprite can be added later. This scene is the weakest picture for walking, so it should be shown as a short fixed scene and a day-to-evening tint (ambient `pulse`) closes the story.
