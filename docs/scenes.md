# Scenes: layout decisions

`src/data/scenes.json` holds the six scenes in story order. Check it with `node scripts/check-scenes.mjs`: it prints `PASS scenes: 6 scenes, all positions valid` or the first 20 problems. All coordinates are in the 640x360 game space. Every person position (`spawn`, `friend`, `friend2`, `extras`) is the point where the character's feet touch the floor (sprite origin bottom centre), so it must lie inside a walk rectangle, and a 32x32 sprite stands in the box from (x-16, y-32) to (x+16, y). `facing` is `down`, `left`, `right` or `up`. Rectangles in `walk` limit where Ric's feet may go; together they form one connected area (rectangles overlap or touch), and the door touches it. The positions were checked by drawing the rectangles, door, spawn and people over the background PNGs at 2x. F9 in a dev or test build draws the same overlay in the running game: walk rectangles green, door red, spawn blue, friends yellow, extras magenta.

Schema beyond the contract, all optional except where stated. `title` (string, the Italian place name shown briefly on arrival) is required. `spawn` also has a `facing`. `door` is `null` in `divano` and otherwise `{x,y,w,h}` with an optional `draw: true`, which asks the flow to draw a plain door panel over the rectangle because the picture has no real door there (only `giuggiu`). `friend` is `null` in `divano`. `friend2` (circe only) is Giusy, the second friend who joins in that scene. `minigame2` (circe only) is `mash`, the second minigame after `d20`. An extra has `personId`, a position and `facing`, and one of: `egg` (an id of `easterEggs` in `dialogue.it.json`, played as a dialogue when Ric talks to it), `intro: true` (Prof. Pinolo: the flow plays the lecture lines of `meet` when Ric talks to him) or `sfx: "meow"` (Gatto di Paz only meows). An extra may also have `tint` (`#rrggbb`) so one sprite can stand in for several passersby; the three egg extras use the `sconosciuta` sprite with a different tint because the cast has no sprite for a student, a dog walker and a barista. `ambient` entries are `{ type, preset, area: {x,y,w,h}, ... }`: type `particles` with preset `dust`, `pollen`, `steam` or `sparkle` (optional `count`); type `tween` with preset `flicker`, `sway` or `pulse` (optional `color`, `alpha: [min,max]`, `period` in ms), meant as a tinted rectangle or a gentle sway of the area. Presets are the vocabulary the ambient code must implement; an unknown preset is rejected by the validator.

Leaving a room (all six are tile rooms, so the exit is the map point `door`). Once the scene is done (the friend joined) Ric leaves by walking into the door rectangle, or by pressing P at a wall when he has the portal gun (below), and both end the scene the same way: the same transition and the next scene. `"exit": "edge"` (optional, `giardini` has it) adds a second way out for a room whose door is not drawn: once the scene is done, walking to any edge of the walkable map (Ric's feet within 4 px of a limit of the map) leaves it. `divano` is left through the credits, whatever its flags. `node scripts/check-scenes.mjs` checks that every door is reachable from the spawn; the e2e test leaves `giardini` by the edge.

Litter bins and the portal gun. `bins` (required in every scene) is a list of `{ x, y }` in pixels, the bin's feet. Standing within 40 px of one and pressing X checks it. `"draw": false` means the room map already draws that bin (`universita`, `giardini`), so the flow draws nothing and the tile under it is blocked; every other bin is drawn in code (`src/game/flow/props.ts`) on a free tile next to a wall, away from the spawn, the door and the people, and Ric cannot walk into it. The first bin Ric checks in a run holds Rick Sanchez's portal gun (`easterEggs.portalFound`, then `run.portalGun` is true until the run is reset); the other bins say one of the `easterEggs.binEmpty` lines at random. With the gun, P at a wall opens a green swirling portal on the wall Ric faces (within about 24 px), Ric and the party walk in and the scene ends as through the door. P before the scene is done says `easterEggs.portalNotYet` instead; in `divano` Ric is never free to walk, so P has no effect there. `divano` has a bin only to keep the rule that every scene has one.

Keys and the menu. Shift runs (1.8 times the walking speed, a faster walk animation; the party follows Ric's trail). Esc opens the menu (`src/game/scenes/MenuScene.ts`) over the paused world scene while Ric is free: Diario (one page per scene reached, `diary.<sceneId>`, a check mark once its friend joined), Comandi (`controls.rows`, plus `controls.portal` once the gun is found), Esci al menu principale (asks "Sei sicuro?", default No; Sì returns to the title with a fresh run) and Indietro. Arrows or WASD and X choose, Esc or Z go back. Menu labels are under `menu` in `dialogue.it.json`.

Debug: `installDebug(game)` in `src/game/debug/index.ts` does nothing in a production build and the bundle contains no debug code. In the dev server and in `VITE_TEST_BUILD=1` builds it listens to the keys F1 to F6 (jump to scene 1 to 6, `debug:jump` with `{ sceneIndex }`), F7 and F8 (win and lose the running minigame, `debug:minigame` with `{ won }`) and F9 (overlay). The flow must listen to the two events on `game.events`; the overlay reads `run.sceneIndex` from `game.registry` and draws on the active scene with key `world`.

**universita.** The scene id stays `universita` but the place is the Cinema Bellinzona, a cinema in Bologna where Prof. Pinolo gives his lessons (display name `title` in `scenes.json`, and the story text of `dialogue.it.json`). The room is done: a tile world of 22 x 14 tiles built from the two photos of the cinema (see the Cinema Bellinzona section of `docs/tiles.md`): the front wall with the big screen over a low stage at the top, six rows of red velvet seats full of people in two blocks, side aisles and exit doors with green signs, round wall lights. The scene is a tile room (`"map": "universita"`, spawn, door and people from the map points, no walk rectangle). Ric starts at the left exit door (tile 1,5) and walks right along the front aisle, Lollino sits in the first seat of the front row (tile 3,6) two tiles on, Prof. Pinolo stands on the stage (tile 6,4), the student extra stands in the right aisle (tile 20,9), and the exit is the matching door of the right wall (tiles 20,5 to 20,6). The litter bin is the recycling bins in the back right corner (`bins` x 656, y 412, `draw: false`). The map also has the interactable points `obj_screen` (tiles 7,1,8,3) and `obj_seats` (tiles 12,6,7,1), which the game code can read. The old lecture hall picture (`bg-universita`, the amphitheatre) is only the background of the minigame `simon`.

**giuggiu.** The room has a parquet floor across the lower middle, three overlapping rectangles that go around the glass table and its chairs. Giuggiu stands to the right of the table near the shelves at (470,300) and Gatto di Paz sits at the edge of the red sofa (330,334) so that the sofa reads as its seat. The picture has no door: the wall between the left wardrobe and the window is flat and empty, so the door is a drawn panel there (`draw: true`) reached by a pocket of floor at its foot, and the small rack that stands in front of that wall is simply covered by the panel. Ric enters from the left at (210,322). The floor is thin (about 22 pixels of free depth in front of the table), so the party train needs the two-row strip in the middle; it is the tightest scene after circe and divano.

**giardini.** All the grass is walkable except the trunks. The thin trunks at x 94-110, 150-170 and 198-208 are avoided by three rectangles: a full-width strip along the bottom, a large block on the right of the trunks and a small block between the first and the third trunk. The big trunk on the right edge (x 600-632) blocks that side, so the exit is the left edge of the park and the door is a 18x40 rectangle at the screen edge with no drawn door. Ric enters at (60,330), next to the exit, which means the player walks away from the exit to meet Liscio at (400,278), near the open sunlit grass in the middle where the slackline will be. The dog walker extra stands at (520,300).

**circe.** The photo is nearly all dinner table and chairs, so the only open floor is the light tile strip at the lower right, 200x38 pixels, between the chair legs and the metal bowl in the corner. Ros (500,308) and Giusy (566,314) stand on it side by side, both facing left toward Ric, who enters at (452,322). The white door at the back left is behind the table and cannot be reached, so the exit is the right edge of the room (a 16x34 rectangle at the screen edge, no drawn door). The silhouettes baked into the picture (five blurred people and the one standing at the right) stay as the background crowd; there are no extras in this scene. This is the weakest layout: the player can move only about 200 pixels, which is enough for two minigame hosts and a short walk.

**cortile.** The venue is dark, and the floor is the crowd band along the bottom, so a wide strip of 600x60 pixels is walkable (the crowd heads simply look like a dark floor) with a short column of 48x40 that leads up to the lit staircase on the left, the door. The staircase is at x 138-178, y 170-254, an orange lit pillar, and the picture shows it clearly. Lara stands in the crowd in front of the stage at (330,304) facing up, the barista extra at (470,306), and Ric enters at (190,300) close to the stairs, which gives a long walk to Lara across the venue. The stage lights pulse in the stage area.

**divano.** The final scene has no door and no friend: the flow plays `meet`, the easter eggs, `join` and then the credits. The photo has almost no floor, because the table fills the bottom and the sofa the middle, so the walkable area is a thin strip, 450x36, in the shadow gap in front of the sofa and over the grey foreground object at its left, from x 30 to x 480. The characters stand in front of the sofa with their feet at y 286-322; the party trails to the left of Ric, who starts at (330,302). The glass balcony door at the left is a good place for the girl upstairs (`sconosciuta`) to enter, but no extra is placed there because the `ric-sopra` egg is played by the flow without her sprite; an optional sprite can be added later. This scene is the weakest picture for walking, so it should be shown as a short fixed scene and a day-to-evening tint (ambient `pulse`) closes the story. This paragraph describes the old picture: `divano` is now a tile room (`"map": "divano"`, spawn from the map point, no walk rectangle, no ambient entries because the map's lights drive the effects), see the divano section of `docs/tiles.md`.
