# Scenes: layout decisions

`src/data/scenes.json` holds the five playable scenes in story order (`universita`, `giuggiu`, `giardini`, `circe`, `cortile`; the old night scene `divano` is gone from the list: after the Cortile the game goes straight to the `ending` scene, whose credits use `src/data/maps/divano.json` as their backdrop). Check it with `node scripts/check-scenes.mjs`: it prints `PASS scenes: 5 scenes, all positions valid` or the first 20 problems. All coordinates are in the 640x360 game space. Every person position (`spawn`, `friend`, `friend2`, `extras`) is the point where the character's feet touch the floor (sprite origin bottom centre), so it must lie inside a walk rectangle, and a 32x32 sprite stands in the box from (x-16, y-32) to (x+16, y). `facing` is `down`, `left`, `right` or `up`. Rectangles in `walk` limit where Ric's feet may go; together they form one connected area (rectangles overlap or touch), and the door touches it. The positions were checked by drawing the rectangles, door, spawn and people over the background PNGs at 2x. F9 in a dev or test build draws the same overlay in the running game: walk rectangles green, door red, spawn blue, friends yellow, extras magenta.

Round 4 additions to the schema. A person may have `"seat": true`: his map point is the blocked tile of a chair (Giuggiu on the black chair of the glass table), the person is drawn in front of the chair and keeps his facing. A bin has `item` (see Litter bins below). A person whose sheet is not in the game yet (`frangetta`, `slackgirl1` to `slackgirl3`, `albanese1`, `albanese2`, `losco` until the art lands) stands as `sconosciuta` in a colour of his own (`STAND_IN` in `WorldScene.ts`); `check-scenes` lists them in `PENDING_ART` until `people.json` has them. Special extras are told apart by `personId`: `frangetta` (a yes/no choice for her number), `losco` (walks up to Lara and makes his offer), `albanese1` and `albanese2` (start at the Cortile bar and walk over to Ros and Giusy); `ros` and `giusy` with the lads are not in `scenes.json`, the flow makes them from the train. The map points a story needs are checked too: `albanese1`, `albanese2`, the four `wall_*` points and the zone `trig_bar` in the Cortile, `obj_plates` and `obj_tv` in Giuggiu's room, and `obj_exitdoor` of the cinema must not cover the real exit `door`.

Schema beyond the contract, all optional except where stated. `title` (string, the Italian place name shown briefly on arrival) is required. `spawn` also has a `facing`. `door` is `null` in `divano` and otherwise `{x,y,w,h}` with an optional `draw: true`, which asks the flow to draw a plain door panel over the rectangle because the picture has no real door there (only `giuggiu`). `friend` is `null` in `divano`. `friend2` (circe only) is Giusy, the second friend who joins in that scene. `minigame2` (circe only) is `mash`, the second minigame after `d20`. An extra has `personId`, a position and `facing`, and one of: `egg` (an id of `easterEggs` in `dialogue.it.json`, played as a dialogue when Ric talks to it), `intro: true` (Prof. Pinolo: the flow plays the lecture lines of `meet` when Ric talks to him) or `sfx: "meow"` (Gatto di Paz only meows). An extra may also have `tint` (`#rrggbb`) so one sprite can stand in for several passersby (the picture rooms only; no scene uses it now). `ambient` entries are `{ type, preset, area: {x,y,w,h}, ... }`: type `particles` with preset `dust`, `pollen`, `steam` or `sparkle` (optional `count`); type `tween` with preset `flicker`, `sway` or `pulse` (optional `color`, `alpha: [min,max]`, `period` in ms), meant as a tinted rectangle or a gentle sway of the area. Presets are the vocabulary the ambient code must implement; an unknown preset is rejected by the validator.

Leaving a room (all five are tile rooms, so the exit is the map point `door`). Once the scene is done (the friend joined) Ric leaves by walking into the door rectangle, or by pressing Z at a wall when he has the portal gun (below), and both end the scene the same way: the same transition and the next scene (after the Cortile, the `ending`). At the Cortile the exit also waits for Ros and Giusy to be back in the train (see the cortile paragraph). `"exit": "edge"` (optional, `giardini` has it) adds a second way out for a room whose door is not drawn: once the scene is done, walking to any edge of the walkable map (Ric's feet within 4 px of a limit of the map) leaves it. `node scripts/check-scenes.mjs` checks that every door is reachable from the spawn; the e2e test leaves `giardini` by the edge.

Litter bins, their items and the portal gun. `bins` (required in every scene) is a list of `{ x, y, item }` in pixels, the bin's feet, with the `item` the bin holds, one per room: `universita` `appunti` (a student's notes on the pinhole camera, the quiz reads the flag), `giuggiu` `portalGun`, `giardini` `peroni` (a closed Peroni, then a yes/no "Bevi la Peroni?": yes sets `peroniBevuta`, which the slack minigame reads and which turns the fringe girl's number scene into a burp), `circe` `fumo` (hash in cling film: with it Ric has an extra exchange with Ros before the d20 roll, which reads the flag for +2), `cortile` `soldi` (20 EUR: it buys the round for Ros and Giusy at the bar). Each item is found once per run (a flag of `run.flags`, `portalGun` is its own field); a second look says one of the `easterEggs.binTaken` lines. `check-scenes` wants the right item in every room. The items are told apart in `dialogue.it.json` by the `easterEggs` keys `appuntiFound`, `portalFound`, `peroniFound`, `fumoFound`, `soldiFound`. Standing within 40 px of a bin and pressing X checks it. Standing within 40 px of one and pressing X checks it. `"draw": false` means the room map already draws that bin (`universita`, `giardini`), so the flow draws nothing and the tile under it is blocked; every other bin is drawn in code (`src/game/flow/props.ts`) on a free tile next to a wall, away from the spawn, the door and the people, and Ric cannot walk into it. The bin of Giuggiu's room holds Rick Sanchez's portal gun (`easterEggs.portalFound`, then `run.portalGun` is true until the run is reset), usable in every room after that. With the gun, Z at a wall (free walk only; Z keeps its other meanings in the dialogue box, where holding it types faster, and in the menus, where it goes back) opens a green swirling portal on the wall Ric faces (within about 24 px), Ric and the party walk in and the scene ends as through the door. Z before the scene is done says `easterEggs.portalNotYet` instead (`easterEggs.portalGirls` at the Cortile while Ros and Giusy are still at the bar).

Choices and the scene jump. A yes/no choice (`src/game/ui/Choice.ts`: arrows and X, styled like the menu's Sì/No, No by default) asks "Bevi la Peroni?" and "Chiedi il numero?". The Esc menu has "Vai alla scena": a list of the five scenes; picking one starts it with every earlier minigame counted as won and the earlier friends in the party (`menu:jump` in `src/game/flow/globals.ts`, the same jump as the F keys).

Keys and the menu. Shift runs (1.8 times the walking speed, a faster walk animation; the party follows Ric's trail). Esc opens the menu (`src/game/scenes/MenuScene.ts`) over the paused world scene while Ric is free: Diario (one page per scene reached, `diary.<sceneId>`, a check mark once its friend joined), Vai alla scena (see above), Comandi (`controls.rows`, plus `controls.portal` once the gun is found), Esci al menu principale (asks "Sei sicuro?", default No; Sì returns to the title with a fresh run) and Indietro. Arrows or WASD and X choose, Esc or Z go back. Menu labels are under `menu` in `dialogue.it.json`.

Debug: `installDebug(game)` in `src/game/debug/index.ts` does nothing in a production build and the bundle contains no debug code. In the dev server and in `VITE_TEST_BUILD=1` builds it listens to the keys F1 to F5 (jump to scene 1 to 5, `debug:jump` with `{ sceneIndex }`, earlier minigames counted as won), F7 and F8 (win and lose the running minigame, `debug:minigame` with `{ won }`) and F9 (overlay). The flow must listen to the two events on `game.events`; the overlay reads `run.sceneIndex` from `game.registry` and draws on the active scene with key `world`.

**universita.** The scene id stays `universita` but the place is the Cinema Bellinzona, a cinema in Bologna where Prof. Pinolo gives his lessons (display name `title` in `scenes.json`, and the story text of `dialogue.it.json`). The room is done: a tile world of 22 x 14 tiles built from the two photos of the cinema (see the Cinema Bellinzona section of `docs/tiles.md`): the front wall with the big screen over a low stage at the top, six rows of red velvet seats full of people in two blocks, side aisles and exit doors with green signs, round wall lights. The scene is a tile room (`"map": "universita"`, spawn, door and people from the map points, no walk rectangle). Ric starts at the left exit door (tile 1,5) and walks right along the front aisle, Lollino sits in the first seat of the front row (tile 3,6) two tiles on, Prof. Pinolo stands on the stage (tile 6,4), and the exit is the matching door of the right wall (tiles 20,5 to 20,6). The litter bin is the recycling bins in the top right corner (`bins` x 656, y 156, `draw: false`). The map also has the interactable points `obj_screen` (tiles 7,1,8,3) and `obj_seats` (tiles 12,6,7,1); four more, `obj_notice`, `obj_speaker`, `obj_exitdoor` (a look-at spot, not the exit) and `obj_sleeper`, work the same way: X next to the point says `objects.universita.<id>`. The old lecture hall picture (`bg-universita`, the amphitheatre) is only the background of the minigame `simon`.

**giuggiu.** The room has a parquet floor across the lower middle, three overlapping rectangles that go around the glass table and its chairs. Giuggiu stands to the right of the table near the shelves at (470,300) and Gatto di Paz sits at the edge of the red sofa (330,334) so that the sofa reads as its seat. The picture has no door: the wall between the left wardrobe and the window is flat and empty, so the door is a drawn panel there (`draw: true`) reached by a pocket of floor at its foot, and the small rack that stands in front of that wall is simply covered by the panel. Ric enters from the left at (210,322). The floor is thin (about 22 pixels of free depth in front of the table), so the party train needs the two-row strip in the middle; it is the tightest scene after circe and divano. Round 4 (the room is a tile room now, see `docs/tiles.md`): Giuggiu sits on the black chair at the east end of the glass table (`"seat": true`), greets Ric with "Ah regà! Daje moveteve che è pronto", three steaming carbonara plates stand on the table (`obj-carbonara`, a drawn plate until `public/assets/objects/carbonara.png` is on the branch), Armando stands in the room as an extra, the cat object line is Giuggiu's sneeze, Ric naps on the pouf (behind it) until a movement key is pressed, and the minigame intro switches the TV to a green portal glow (16:20, Rick and Morty).

**giardini.** All the grass is walkable except the trunks. The thin trunks at x 94-110, 150-170 and 198-208 are avoided by three rectangles: a full-width strip along the bottom, a large block on the right of the trunks and a small block between the first and the third trunk. The big trunk on the right edge (x 600-632) blocks that side, so the exit is the left edge of the park and the door is a 18x40 rectangle at the screen edge with no drawn door. Ric enters at (60,330), next to the exit, which means the player walks away from the exit to meet Liscio at (400,278), near the open sunlit grass in the middle where the slackline will be. The dog walker extra stands at (520,300). Round 4: she is now `slackgirl1`, a girl who asks whether she can get on the slack, with `slackgirl2` and `slackgirl3` beside the line and `frangetta` (blunt fringe, Birkenstocks) on the grass left of the path; Ric can ask the latter for her number (`numeroFrangetta`), unless he drank the Peroni, in which case he burps and she walks off to the exit. Ric's parents (moved here from `circe`) stand on the grass near the entrance (map points `papa` and `mamma`).

**circe.** The photo is nearly all dinner table and chairs, so the only open floor is the light tile strip at the lower right, 200x38 pixels, between the chair legs and the metal bowl in the corner. Ros (500,308) and Giusy (566,314) stand on it side by side, both facing left toward Ric, who enters at (452,322). The white door at the back left is behind the table and cannot be reached, so the exit is the right edge of the room (a 16x34 rectangle at the screen edge, no drawn door). The silhouettes baked into the picture (five blurred people and the one standing at the right) stay as the background crowd; there are no extras in this scene. This is the weakest layout: the player can move only about 200 pixels, which is enough for two minigame hosts and a short walk.

**cortile.** The venue is dark, and the floor is the crowd band along the bottom, so a wide strip of 600x60 pixels is walkable (the crowd heads simply look like a dark floor) with a short column of 48x40 that leads up to the lit staircase on the left, the door. The staircase is at x 138-178, y 170-254, an orange lit pillar, and the picture shows it clearly. Lara stands in the crowd in front of the stage at (330,304) facing up, the barista extra at (470,306), and Ric enters at (190,300) close to the stairs, which gives a long walk to Lara across the venue. The stage lights pulse in the stage area. Round 4 (see `docs/tiles.md` for the tile room): Armando and Carolina sit at one of the big tables on the right, the hooded man `losco`, and two Albanian lads `albanese1` and `albanese2` at the top end of the bar. When Ric starts talking to Lara, after the line of `meet` marked `"event": "losco"` (the fourth) the hooded man walks up to her side while the narrator says so (`loscoApproach`), makes his offer of "cocco" (`losco`), and the talk with Lara goes on; talking to him first does the same without the rest of the talk, once. A few steps in from the door (the map zone `trig_bar`) Ros and Giusy leave the train and stop where they are; the two lads walk over to them (along the free tiles of the map) while the narrator line and Ric's "Eh vabè, le abbiamo già perse..." play, then all four walk to the bottom right corner, to the points `wall_ros`, `wall_giusy`, `wall_albanese1` and `wall_albanese2`, and each couple stands face to face, one couple above the other. Nobody of the four can be talked to until all have arrived. Talking to Ros or Giusy before the minigame without money gets a "busy" exchange; with the 20 EUR from the bin Ric buys the round ("Meh ragazze, posso offrirvi da bere?"), the lads walk off, Ros calls him a "coglione", Giusy agrees, he answers "Che ingrate" and both walk back into the train (`soldiUsati`). Without money, after the minigame, he fetches them with "Meh... sciamanin?!". The exit (door, edge and portal) stays shut while they are with the lads (`barState` `'away'` in `WorldScene`); then the game goes to the `ending`.
