# Minigames: Simon and Cat-smoke

Both are Phaser scenes in `src/game/minigames/<id>/index.ts` (default export, `static KEY = 'minigame-<id>'`, `static ID = '<id>'`), picked up by the registry glob. Each shows the `InstructionCard` first, reacts to the game event `debug:minigame` ({won}) by finishing at once, ends with a result banner (2 s) and then emits `MINIGAME_DONE` with `{ id, won, score }` and stops itself. Italian texts come from `dialogue.it.json` (`minigames.simon`, `minigames['cat-smoke']`, `ui.win`, `ui.lose`, `ui.confirm`); the tiny HUD words live in `labels.ts` next to each scene.

## Simon (scene universita)

Prof. Pinolo (`char-pinolo`, 2x, stick on the top corner of a chalkboard that shows the round and the hearts) lights the keys of a big arrow pad one at a time (380 ms lit, 190 ms gap) with a distinct tone per direction (G4 left, C5 down, E5 up, G5 right, through `beep()` of `audioContext.ts`, skipped while muted). The player retypes with the arrow keys or WASD; progress shows as squares under the status word. Three rounds of 4, 5 and 6 keys (never three equal keys in a row), 3 hearts. A wrong key, or 8 s without a key, costs a heart, plays `miss` and replays the same sequence; 0 hearts loses, finishing round 3 wins. Score is the number of rounds completed. A full clean run takes about 30 s.

## Cat-smoke (scene giuggiu)

A living room drawn in code (wall, wood floor, rug, window) with three sofas, labelled with arrow icons and SINISTRA / SU / DESTRA (keys LEFT, UP, RIGHT or A, W, D). Phase 1 (about 15 s): the cat (`char-gatto`, 2x) hops between sofas with a small arc, leaving fading paw dots, while translucent smoke clouds (a code-made texture, tweened scale, drift and alpha) often drift over it. It then fakes one duck under a sofa, hops on, and finally slips under a random sofa (the targeted smoke stops before that). Phase 2: a 3 s countdown, press an arrow key to select a sofa (nothing is selected by default) and X, Enter or Space to confirm; at 0 the current selection is confirmed, and with no selection the round is lost. The chosen sofa is lifted: the cat is there (`meow` + `win`) or the floor is bare (`miss` + `lose`, then the right sofa lifts to show the cat). All cat moves are linear and measured at 109 px/s at most (limit 120); a swap takes at least 2 s, far below the limit of 2 per second. Score is 1 for a win, else 0.
