# Minigames: Simon and Cat-smoke

Both are Phaser scenes in `src/game/minigames/<id>/index.ts` (default export, `static KEY = 'minigame-<id>'`, `static ID = '<id>'`), picked up by the registry glob. Each shows the `InstructionCard` first, reacts to the game event `debug:minigame` ({won}) by finishing at once, ends with a result banner (2 s) and then emits `MINIGAME_DONE` with `{ id, won, score }` and stops itself. Italian texts come from `dialogue.it.json` (`minigames.simon`, `minigames['cat-smoke']`, `ui.win`, `ui.lose`, `ui.confirm`); the tiny HUD words live in `labels.ts` next to each scene.

## Simon (scene universita)

Prof. Pinolo (`char-pinolo`, 2x, stick on the top corner of a chalkboard that shows the round and the hearts) lights the keys of a big arrow pad one at a time (380 ms lit, 190 ms gap) with a distinct tone per direction (G4 left, C5 down, E5 up, G5 right, through `audio.tone()`, so mute and volume apply). The player retypes with the arrow keys or WASD; progress shows as squares under the status word. Three rounds of 4, 5 and 6 keys (never three equal keys in a row), 3 hearts. A wrong key, or 8 s without a key, costs a heart, plays `miss` and replays the same sequence; 0 hearts loses, finishing round 3 wins. Score is the number of rounds completed. A full clean run takes about 30 s.

## Cat-smoke (scene giuggiu)

A living room drawn in code (wall, wood floor, rug, window) with three sofas, labelled with arrow icons and SINISTRA / SU / DESTRA (keys LEFT, UP, RIGHT or A, W, D). Giuggiu (`char-giuggiu`, 3x, a cigarette with a glowing ember in his mouth) stands at the left or the right edge of the screen, chosen at random each round, and puffs: every 1.7 s he breathes in (the chest swells), leans toward the room and blows a burst of eight clouds from his mouth, with a thinner trickle in between. The clouds come from one dense soft texture (`cat-smoke-cloud-2`, 0.2 alpha per ring, up to 0.95 opaque), grow to 2.4 to 3.6 times, drift 180 to 640 px toward the far edge and spread over the whole height of the room, so the screen is really covered; they live in one container so the smoke can be thinned with one tween. Phase 1 (about 12 s): the cat (`char-gatto`, 2x) hops between sofas at 200 px/s with a small arc (the first version ran at 100 px/s; a hop takes at least 0.5 s, 0.9 s between neighbours, and the phase is timed so the cat makes about as many hops as before), leaving fading paw dots and a step sound on each landing. It then fakes one duck under a sofa and comes back out. Then Giuggiu stops and the smoke fades to a haze (container alpha 0.3) for the last hop and the hiding, so the end of the chase is always seen, and the cat slips under a random sofa. Phase 2: keys count from the moment the cat is hidden, not only from the countdown: an arrow key selects a sofa (nothing is selected by default) and X, Enter or Space confirms at once; the 3 s countdown starts about 1 s after the hiding, and at 0 the current selection is confirmed (with no selection the round is lost). The chosen sofa is lifted: the cat is there (`meow` + `win`) or the floor is bare (`miss` + `lose`, then the right sofa lifts to show the cat). Score is 1 for a win, else 0.

The "wrong sofa" bug of the first playtest: the scene ignored every key until the countdown began, 1.6 s after the cat had hidden, so a player who picked the right sofa as soon as the cat vanished lost the round with nothing selected. `scripts/e2e.mjs` replays that (picks the sofa the cat is under the moment it hides) and fails unless the round is won.
