# Minigames: Simon and Cat-smoke

Both are Phaser scenes in `src/game/minigames/<id>/index.ts` (default export, `static KEY = 'minigame-<id>'`, `static ID = '<id>'`), picked up by the registry glob. Each shows the `InstructionCard` first, reacts to the game event `debug:minigame` ({won}) by finishing at once, ends with a result banner (2 s) and then emits `MINIGAME_DONE` with `{ id, won, score }` and stops itself. Italian texts come from `dialogue.it.json` (`minigames.simon`, `minigames['cat-smoke']`, `ui.win`, `ui.lose`, `ui.confirm`); the tiny HUD words live in `labels.ts` next to each scene.

## Simon, now the pinhole camera quiz (scene universita)

Round 4 replaced the memory game with a quiz; the minigame id is still `simon` (the folder `src/game/minigames/simon/`, `scenes.json`, the registry and the run results key on it), the scene class is `QuizMinigame`. Three questions about the pinhole camera (foro stenopeico), four answers each, exactly one right: what forms the image (a tiny hole, no lens), how the image looks (inverted, up and down and left and right), what a smaller hole does (sharper but darker, so a longer exposure). The questions, the answers and the one-line explanation shown after each answer are in `simon/labels.ts` (`QUESTIONS`, the right answer first: the scene shuffles the rows each time; the strict key list of `check-dialogue` leaves no room for them in `dialogue.it.json`). Up and down (or W and S) move the cursor, X, Enter or Space confirms. After each answer the right row turns green, a wrong pick red, and the explanation shows for 2.6 s; a pip row at the top right records the three results. All three questions are always asked; 2 right out of 3 win (a score of 2 or 3), 0 or 1 lose. Score is the number of right answers.

With the run flag `appunti` (Ric found a student's crumpled notes in a bin, `getRun(game).flags?.appunti`, read through `minigames/flags.ts`) every wrong answer is drawn dark and greyed, so the right one stands out, and the caption "Appunti stropicciati" shows at the top. The background is `bg-universita`, the render of the cinema room (see below). Test fields on the scene: `correct` (row of the right answer), `sel`, `accepting`, `score`; `scripts/e2e.mjs` plays it with real keys by arrowing to `correct`.

## Backgrounds

`public/assets/backgrounds/<scene>.png` are no longer the pixelised photos: `python -I scripts/art/room_backgrounds.py` renders each one from `src/data/maps/<scene>.json` and `public/assets/tiles/tileset.png` only, the way `tileMap.ts` draws a room (ground, walls, below, objects, above, then the night mood and the steady glow of each light, no people). The result is a native-scale 640x360 window of the room, no resampling; the window of each room is centred on a focus point set in the script (`FOCUS`): the screen of the cinema, the slackline between the two trunks of the Giardini (the slackline minigame walks it and draws no scenery of its own), the stage of the Cortile. Keys and paths are unchanged (`backgrounds/<id>.png`, texture `bg-<id>`). The old script `scripts/art/backgrounds.py` is left alone.

## Cat-smoke (scene giuggiu)

A living room drawn in code (wall, wood floor, rug, window) with three sofas, labelled with arrow icons and SINISTRA / SU / DESTRA (keys LEFT, UP, RIGHT or A, W, D). Giuggiu (`char-giuggiu`, 3x, a cigarette with a glowing ember in his mouth) stands at the very right edge of the screen, below the right sofa (always there since round 4, so the smoke reaches all three sofas), and puffs: every 1.7 s he breathes in (the chest swells), leans toward the room and blows a burst of eight clouds from his mouth, with a thinner trickle in between. The clouds come from one dense soft texture (`cat-smoke-cloud-2`, 0.2 alpha per ring, up to 0.95 opaque), grow to 2.4 to 3.6 times, drift 60 to 640 px toward the far edge (a quarter of them instead rise near Giuggiu, 0 to 120 px, over the sofa on his side) and spread over the whole height of the room, so the screen is really covered; they live in one container so the smoke can be thinned with one tween. Phase 1 (about 12 s): the cat (`char-gatto`, 2x) hops between sofas at 200 px/s with a small arc (the first version ran at 100 px/s; a hop takes at least 0.5 s, 0.9 s between neighbours, and the phase is timed so the cat makes about as many hops as before), leaving fading paw dots and a step sound on each landing. It then fakes one duck under a sofa and comes back out. Then Giuggiu stops and the smoke fades to a haze (container alpha 0.3) for the last hop and the hiding, so the end of the chase is always seen, and the cat slips under a random sofa. Phase 2: keys count from the moment the cat is hidden, not only from the countdown: an arrow key selects a sofa (nothing is selected by default) and X, Enter or Space confirms at once; the 3 s countdown starts about 1 s after the hiding, and at 0 the current selection is confirmed (with no selection the round is lost). The chosen sofa is lifted: the cat is there (`meow` + `win`) or the floor is bare (`miss` + `lose`, then the right sofa lifts to show the cat). Score is 1 for a win, else 0.

The "wrong sofa" bug of the first playtest: the scene ignored every key until the countdown began, 1.6 s after the cat had hidden, so a player who picked the right sofa as soon as the cat vanished lost the round with nothing selected. `scripts/e2e.mjs` replays that (picks the sofa the cat is under the moment it hides) and fails unless the round is won.
